SERVICE 03

Solar Structure Fabrication

سولر پینل کا مضبوط لوہے کا اسٹرکچر

Solar panels are expensive assets that produce power for 25+ years. They require a rigid, heavy-gauge foundation that will not warp or collapse in violent winds. We build custom L2, L3, elevated structures, and dual-pole tracking frames engineered to the exact tilt angle for maximum solar irradiance in Pakistan.

Key Specifications & Benefits:

  • ✓ Materials: 12, 14, & 16-gauge hot-dipped Galvanized Iron (GI) or treated heavy mild steel channel.
  • ✓ Wind Rating: Engineered to withstand wind gusts up to 140 km/h.
  • ✓ Custom Elevation: Standard low profile or 6ft - 9ft elevated roof designs to preserve rooftop walking space.
  • ✓ Foundation Anchoring: Heavy anchor expansion bolts with concrete pad pedestals for ultimate stability.

SERVICE 04

Solar Plate Fitting

سولر پلیٹس کی محفوظ فٹنگ

Mounting bi-facial and mono-PERC solar plates onto iron rails requires specialized clamping so micro-cracks do not develop in the silicon wafers. Our technicians align each panel with aerospace precision using heavy aluminum mid-clamps, end-clamps, spring washers, and vibration dampers.

Key Specifications & Benefits:

  • ✓ Micro-Crack Protection: Calibrated tightening torque protects panel tempered glass.
  • ✓ Rust-Proof Fasteners: Stainless steel 304 or galvanized grade 8.8 bolts.
  • ✓ Anti-Theft Locking: Security sheer nuts and lock clips available on request.
  • ✓ Clean Aesthetic: Uniform spacing, zero shadow casting, and structured under-panel cable trays.

Bike Bhatti Painting
